Contract Key Clause & Expiry Auditor:
Contract Key Clause & Expiry Auditor is an AI-powered automation project designed to simplify the process of reviewing and monitoring legal contracts and Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). The system automatically retrieves contracts from Google Drive, analyzes their contents, and extracts important information such as renewal/expiry deadlines, liability limits, termination terms, and other key contractual clauses.
The extracted information is organized and processed automatically, reducing the need for manual contract review. The system can also create Google Calendar reminders for upcoming contract expiry or renewal dates, helping users avoid missed deadlines.

Key Features:
📄 Automatically process contracts and NDAs from Google Drive
🤖 Use AI to identify and extract important contract clauses
📅 Detect expiry and renewal deadlines
⚖️ Extract liability limits and termination conditions
🔔 Create Google Calendar reminders for important dates
⚡ Reduce manual contract-review effort
📊 Provide structured and easy-to-understand contract information

Workflow:
Google Drive → Contract Extraction → AI Clause Analysis → Key Information Extraction → Expiry/Renewal Detection → Google Calendar Reminder

Objective:
The main objective of this project is to automate contract auditing and deadline monitoring, making it easier for organizations to track important legal obligations and take timely action before contracts expire or require renewal.
